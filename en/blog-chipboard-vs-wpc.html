German chipboard or WPC wood substitute? Price, durability & humidity compared

Published 2026-11-13 — the Sharqia Paints & Decor team

The question we hear most when clients request wall decor: should I choose German chipboard or wood substitute slats? There is no absolute winner — there is a right choice for each place. This comparison comes from installing both together across dozens of projects:

Comparison pointGerman chipboard 18mmRibbed WPC wood substitute
Material & thicknessCompressed fibre boards with wood veneer, 18mmRibbed wood-plastic composite slats
Water & humidity resistanceGood for air humidity; not for direct wettingExcellent — suits areas around washbasins & bathrooms
Look & feelFlat, luxurious, natural wood feelModern ribbed lines with a light-and-shadow rhythm
Best useTV walls, entrances, full claddingCeilings, partial walls, humid areas
Concealed lightingExcellent with mirrors and edgesExcellent inside slat grooves
Relative costMedium to high depending on veneerMedium, and faster to install

Our professional call in most homes

The main TV wall: chipboard, for its visual value. The ceiling and the wall near a washbasin or bathroom: WPC, for its resistance. And many of our projects combine both on one wall — flat chipboard broken up by WPC slats and LED lighting, as you can see in the gallery.

A warning before buying from the market yourself

Chipboard thickness is the key difference: cheap 12mm boards warp within months in Eastern Province humidity. Insist on 18mm, and on seeing the actual sample, not a catalogue photo.
