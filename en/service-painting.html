Frequently asked questions

How much does painting cost per metre in Dammam and Khobar?

As a market estimate, painting new plaster runs about SAR 12–25 per m² including materials, and repainting with scraping and treatment about SAR 18–35. Your final price is confirmed after the free inspection, based on wall condition and paint type — see our Prices page.

Which paint best withstands Eastern Province humidity?

For facades we recommend profile systems such as Jotun Jotashield; indoors, Jotun Fenomastic or Al Jazeera Green — provided the humidity source is treated and the surface prepared first. Excellent paint on a damp wall will fail whatever its brand.

Do you guarantee the paintwork?

Yes — a written guarantee on workmanship, against peeling and colour change, when the agreed preparation plan is followed.

How long does painting an apartment or villa take?

One room usually takes 1–2 days, a mid-size apartment 3–5 days, and villas depend on area and number of coats — the schedule is fixed in writing in your quote.
