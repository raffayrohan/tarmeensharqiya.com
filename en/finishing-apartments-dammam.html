Apartment finishing stages, in the right order

  • 1. First fix: plumbing, electrical and concealed AC — any mistake here costs double later.
  • 2. Plaster & putty: wall preparation with Saveto putty to full flatness.
  • 3. Ceilings: gypsum board on a 0.6mm frame with concealed and magnetic lighting layout.
  • 4. Floors & bathrooms: porcelain, waterproofing and sanitary ware installation.
  • 5. Paints & decor: final paint, chipboard or wallpaper on feature areas.
  • 6. Snagging & handover: item-by-item check with you and a clean handover.

How long does apartment finishing take?

As a general market reference, a mid-size apartment takes two to three weeks when the first fix is sound, extending with plumbing changes or bespoke decor. Your actual schedule is fixed in writing in the contract before we start.

Why one contractor beats separate tradesmen

Because clashing stages are the biggest cause of delay and waste: the painter waits for the gypsum installer, who waits for the electrician. With us there is one accountable sequence, and one total price known from day one within the ranges of our price guide.
