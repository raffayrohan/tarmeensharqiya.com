Frequently asked questions

Why is your guarantee 10 years when others give 3–5?

Because we use Korean PVC at 1100gsm and a fully galvanised, engineered structure. Heavier fabric and a stronger frame mean a longer life — and we put that in a written guarantee.

What is the best car shade fabric in Dammam and Khobar?

Korean PVC at 1100gsm is the best choice for coastal heat and humidity: it does not stretch or sag over time like light fabrics, and it withstands UV.

Can you make a custom laser-cut screen design?

Yes — CNC laser cutting in ready-made or custom designs, with oven or powder coating resistant to rust.

How long does a car shade take?

After size and design approval, fabrication and installation usually take 4–8 days depending on the number of bays and structure type.
