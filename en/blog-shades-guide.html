Car shades in the Eastern Province: what makes one shade live 10 years and another sag in two?

Published 2026-11-15 — the Sharqia Paints & Decor team

Many car shades in Dammam and Khobar look identical on installation day; two summers later, some fabrics sag and some structures rust. The difference hides in three numbers and specifications most clients never ask about:

1. Fabric weight: 1100gsm is not a luxury

Korean PVC fabric at 1100 g/m² is heavier and denser than the common fabrics (600–800gsm). Higher density means better resistance to UV and heat above 50°C, and constant tension with no sagging — the basis of our ability to offer a 10-year guarantee while the market norm is 3–5 years.

2. The structure: galvanised, or just painted?

The salty Eastern Province air eats painted steel from the first scratch. A galvanised structure with rust-resistant primer lives many times longer. Ask any contractor: is the steel galvanised or black and painted? His answer tells you your shade's lifespan.

3. Fixing and slope

  • Footings and fixing calculated for seasonal Eastern Province wind direction, not just static weight.
  • A studied slope that drains rainwater away from the car and entrance.
  • Balanced fabric tension that prevents water pooling on top — the number one shade killer.

Before you sign with any shade contractor

Ask for three things in writing: the fabric's weight and country of origin, the structure type and its treatment, and the guarantee period and what it covers (fabric only, or structure and installation too?). Those three lines in a quote are worth more than any discount.
