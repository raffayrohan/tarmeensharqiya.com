Eastern Province humidity: how to protect your walls and roof

Published 2026-11-10 — the Sharqia Paints & Decor team

Homes in Dammam, Khobar, Qatif and Ras Tanura live with a silent enemy: high air humidity and sea salt that creep into walls and roofs, then suddenly appear as peeling paint, yellow stains and a musty smell. The good news: most of these problems are preventable with the right steps before they start.

Why do north walls peel specifically?

North walls are the home's coldest surfaces, so indoor water vapour condenses on them — especially behind furniture placed flush against the wall, where air cannot circulate. Over time putty and paint saturate with water and peel. The cure is not new paint over old, but: scraping to a sound base, full drying, salt treatment, a sealing primer, then paint.

Roof insulation before the rainy season

  • Check falls, expansion joints and rain outlets first — most leaks come from details, not the roof area itself.
  • Treat cracks and repair blistered plaster.
  • Apply a full acrylic or elastomeric insulation system — the system we back with a written 10-year guarantee.

Signs to call now, not later

  • Yellow stains growing on a top-floor ceiling after every rain.
  • Peeling that recurs in the same spot despite repainting.
  • A damp smell in a closed room or behind a wardrobe.
  • Blown putty at the base of ground-floor walls (rising damp).

The earlier the problem is treated, the cheaper and shorter the repair. Our inspection is free, and we identify the humidity source before proposing any fix.
