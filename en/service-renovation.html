Frequently asked questions

How much does villa renovation cost in the Eastern Province?

General renovation is estimated at SAR 250–450 per m² depending on condition and finishes; luxury finishing can reach SAR 500–900 per m². An exact figure only follows inspection of the building and its services — and the inspection is free.

How long does renovating an apartment or villa take?

As a general market reference, apartment finishing takes 2–3 weeks and larger villas one to two months — your actual schedule is fixed in the contract for your scope.

Do you build rooftop and external annexes?

Yes — external annexes, majlis buildings and rooftop annexes in steel structures with cement-board cladding or blockwork, with full finishing and insulation.

Is roof insulation guaranteed?

Yes — our acrylic and elastomeric insulation systems carry a written 10-year guarantee against leaks when the full system is applied.
