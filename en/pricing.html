What actually moves the price?

  • Surface condition: a new wall is not a peeling wall needing scraping and humidity and salt treatment.
  • Material type: Jotun Fenomastic is not an economy grade, and German 18mm chipboard is not a light board.
  • Number of layers: 3 coats of Saveto putty and two paint coats differ from one quick coat.
  • Height & access: high facades need scaffolding and safety equipment.
  • Guarantee: work guaranteed in writing is priced with higher responsibility — and saves you re-doing it later.

And what about shades and screens?

Almost no contractor in the market publishes a fixed range for shades, because price depends on size, fabric type and structure. What we commit to: Korean PVC fabric at 1100gsm, a galvanised structure, and a written price after a free inspection — with a 10-year guarantee while the market norm is 3–5 years.

Frequently asked questions

Are these your final prices?

No — these are published indicative averages from the Eastern Province market, to help you plan. Your final price is set after the free inspection and handed to you written and itemised by materials and items before any work starts.

Why do prices differ between contractors for the same job?

Three factors: material type (named originals vs substitutes), preparation quality (full scraping and treatment vs painting over the old), and whether a written guarantee exists. A fair comparison uses the same specification.

Are prices per metre or lump sum?

Standard painting and finishing are per square metre; special work such as doors and decor is lump sum — the method is stated clearly in your quote.
