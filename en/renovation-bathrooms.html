Bathroom renovation from demolition to handover

The bathroom is where the difference between proper renovation and patching shows most: waterproofing flood-tested before tiling, fully renewed plumbing where needed, and porcelain and LED mirrors to a hotel standard.

What complete bathroom renovation includes

  • Demolition and removal of old tiles and sanitary ware, with debris removal.
  • Inspecting and renewing plumbing and drainage — the number one cause of bathroom leaks.
  • Full waterproofing of floors and wet walls, flood-tested before tiling.
  • Porcelain or marble for floors and walls in modern large formats.
  • Fitting sanitary ware, glass shower enclosures and LED mirrors.
