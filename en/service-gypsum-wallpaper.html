Frequently asked questions

Why does gypsum board crack after a while?

Most cracks come from frames thinner than 0.6mm or skipped joint tape. We use a 0.6mm galvanised frame and fully treated joints, so the ceiling stays flat with no visible cracks.

Is there water-resistant gypsum board?

Yes — green moisture-resistant boards from Mada and Knauf are made for bathrooms and kitchens, and we install them with proper waterproofing behind wet zones.

Can wallpaper go over a painted wall?

Yes, if the paint is sound and not peeling. We prepare the surface with sanding and a suitable primer, then install Korean or German wallpaper with near-invisible seams.

How long does a room or hall ceiling take?

A mid-size hall ceiling usually takes 3–5 days including frame, boards, compound and paint, depending on design and lighting complexity.
