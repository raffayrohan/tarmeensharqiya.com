Frequently asked questions

What is the difference between German chipboard and WPC wood substitute?

Chipboard is flat 18mm board suited to large feature walls and cladding with a natural wood feel. WPC slats are ribbed, lighter and far more water-resistant, suiting ceilings and walls near humidity. Full comparison in our blog article.

Does chipboard withstand Eastern Province humidity?

German 18mm chipboard is treated and handles normal indoor humidity well. For areas touching water — around washbasins, for example — we recommend WPC or marble substitute.

Can you build a TV wall with hidden cables?

Yes — we design the wall with internal channels hiding TV, receiver and lighting cables, plus storage units on request.

How long does one feature wall take?

After measurements and materials are approved, fabrication and installation usually take 3–7 days depending on wall size, slats and lighting.
